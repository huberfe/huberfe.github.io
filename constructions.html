General constructions

Let q be an arbitrary prime power. Then for all 3 <= n <= q and 1 <= d <= n/2 + 1 there exist quantum MDS codes [[n, n - 2d + 2, d]]_q.
Ref: Thm. 14 in Grassl et al., "On optimal quantum codes", quant-ph/0312164

Thus if q is a prime power and 3<=n<=q, an AME(n,q) state exists.

AME(n,D) exist in all dimensions n=2,3,5,6:
n=2,3: correspond to Bell and GHZ states.
n=5,6: prime dimensions D can be obtained from the graph states presented in Fig. 2 of below reference, non-prime dimension are obtained by tensoring AME states according to the prime-factorisation of D.

For n=4, AME(n,D) exist for all odd D (from the graph state of Fig. 3 in below reference).
All dimensions D that have even number of 2's in the prime-factorisation of D are obtained by taking copies of graph states in Fig. 3 and Fig. 7.
Ref: Fig. 3 and Fig. 7 in W. Helwig, "Absolutely Maximally Entangled Qudit Graph States", arXiv:1306.2879

For n=5 or n=6, this was already presented by Rains:
There exist AME(n,D) states for all D: For all integers D > 1, there exists a ((5, D, 3))_D and a ((6, 1, 4))_D code.
Ref: Theorem 13 in E. M. Rains, "Nonbinary quantum codes" (quant-ph/9703048)

For n=5, a construction for AME(5,D) can also be found in
Ref: Prop. 3 in Goyeneche et al., "Entanglement and quantum combinatorial designs", arXiv:1708.05946

AME states of minimal support are equivalent to classical MDS codes.
D. Alsina, "PhD thesis: Multipartite entanglement and quantum algorithms", (arXiv:1706.08318)

AME of 4 parties correspond to MOLS (or Quantum MOLS), of 6 parties correspond to MOL Cubes, 8 parties to MOL Hypercubes, etc., where MOL = mutually orthogonal Latin squares
K. Życzkowski, private communication (2019)
