How to cite

If you use these results in your research, please cite them as:

F. Huber and N. Wyderka,
"Table of AME states",
Online available at http://www.tp.nt.uni-siegen.de/+fhuber/ame.html.


Naturally, we would be pleased if you also cite the companion paper in which these bounds first appeared (Fig. 2):

F. Huber, C. Eltschka, J. Siewert and O. Gühne,
"Bounds on absolutely maximally entangled states from shadow inequalities, and the quantum MacWilliams identity",
J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 51 175301 (2018),
https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/aaade5,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.06298


Comments, corrections, and updates are welcome!
We thank Daniel Alsina, Dardo Goyeneche, Markus Grassl, and Karol Życzkowski for comments.
